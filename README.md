\# Priyanka Bhatt



I am pursuing a B.Tech Honours degree in Data Science, with a specialization in Deep Learning and a minor in Psychology.



I chose this combination deliberately. I have always been drawn to both technical systems and human behaviour, and I see them as deeply connected — especially in the way AI systems are designed, used, trusted, and questioned.



I care about building AI systems that are technically useful, human-aware, and grounded in real problems.



\---



Before I write code, I often start by drawing something: a diagram, a flow, or a rough map of how the pieces relate.



Visualizing the full picture helps me understand the problem before committing to a direction. I like working through ambiguity, breaking systems down, and finding the structure underneath messy real-world data or workflows.

\## What I think about AI



AI is something I find genuinely fascinating, but also something that needs to be worked with carefully and deliberately.



I am interested not only in what AI can do, but also in where it fails, what assumptions it carries, and how it affects the people who use it.
